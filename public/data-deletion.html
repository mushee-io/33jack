Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 9 October 2026

You can request deletion of personal information associated with your use of 33JACK, including information received through connected channels such as WhatsApp.

How to request deletion

Email contact@mushee.xyz with the subject 33JACK Data Deletion Request.

Include enough information for us to identify the relevant account or workspace. For a WhatsApp request, include the phone number used with 33JACK in international format. Do not send passwords, access tokens or full bank-account credentials.

What happens next

  • We may ask you to verify that you control the relevant account or channel.
  • Once verified, we will delete or anonymise personal information that is no longer required for security, fraud prevention, legal, audit or compliance purposes.
  • Where data must be retained for a lawful reason, we will restrict its use to that purpose where reasonably possible.
  • Information held independently by third-party services may also be subject to those providers' own deletion procedures.

Disconnecting WhatsApp

You may also stop interacting with 33JACK on WhatsApp and remove access through the relevant Meta/WhatsApp account controls. A platform disconnection does not automatically erase records that 33JACK must retain for legitimate security or legal reasons, so use the email request above if you also want deletion.

Contact

For questions, contact contact@mushee.xyz.
