Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

33JACK is a business finance-operations product operated by Mushee. This policy explains how information is handled when you use 33JACK on the web or through connected messaging channels such as WhatsApp and Telegram.

Information we may process

  • Account and channel identifiers, such as phone numbers, WhatsApp identifiers, Telegram identifiers and workspace membership data.
  • Messages, commands and files you send to 33JACK, including invoice images, PDFs and voice messages.
  • Invoice and payment information extracted from documents, such as supplier names, invoice references, currencies, amounts, due dates and payout instructions.
  • Limited beneficiary and transaction metadata used for risk checks, reconciliation and audit records. Where possible, 33JACK stores limited fingerprints rather than complete banking credentials.
  • Technical and security information such as request logs, webhook identifiers, timestamps, approval records and risk events.

How we use information

We use information to provide invoice analysis, payment preparation, fraud and duplicate checks, workspace access control, human approval flows, settlement testing, reconciliation, support, security and service improvement. AI may prepare or explain a payment instruction, but 33JACK keeps money movement inside structured approval controls.

Service providers

33JACK may use infrastructure and service providers to operate the product, including Meta/WhatsApp, Telegram, Vercel, database providers, AI inference providers, blockchain infrastructure and payout or banking sandbox providers. These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, audit and improve the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements. Test and sandbox records may be retained for debugging and product development.

Security

We use technical controls such as HTTPS, signed approval tokens, webhook-signature validation, role checks and restricted access to reduce unauthorised access. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and deletion

You may request access, correction or deletion of information associated with your 33JACK use. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

Children

33JACK is designed for business users and is not intended for children.

International processing

Because 33JACK uses global cloud and messaging infrastructure, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you are located, subject to applicable safeguards.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact contact@mushee.xyz.
